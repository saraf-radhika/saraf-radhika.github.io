01 — About

Fifteen years turning complex problems into architecture people can build and trust.

I'm a Vice President and Solution Architect at Barclays. I set up and run solution architecture for the pre-trade markets business, and I lead the work on adopting AI responsibly across that domain.

My career started with writing automation scripts in India. From there I moved to architecting cloud and identity platforms in Australia, then to leading architecture teams in the UK. Most of my work is translation: taking a business problem, a set of regulatory constraints and a long-term roadmap, and turning them into something teams can build and run.
