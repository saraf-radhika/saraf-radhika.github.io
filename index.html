About

I design the systems that sit behind trading desks, payment rails and customer onboarding at large banks, and I help teams adopt AI in a way regulators and risk partners can trust.

Over fifteen years I've moved from writing automation scripts in India, to architecting cloud and identity platforms in Australia, to leading architecture practices in the UK. Today I'm a Vice President at Barclays, where I set up and run solution architecture for the pre-trade markets business.

Most of my work is translation: taking a business problem, a set of regulatory constraints and a long-term technology roadmap, and turning them into an architecture people can actually build and run.
